2. Purpose and Official Affiliation

UniTrack is an independent, unofficial student application designed to help students in Afghanistan access and review academic information available through the HEMIS platform and use student-result calculation and analysis features.

Unofficial application notice: UniTrack is not an official HEMIS application. It is independently developed and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by HEMIS, the Ministry of Higher Education of Afghanistan, or any university.

The HEMIS platform is available at https://hemis.edu.af/ . HEMIS is the source of academic information accessed through that platform; UniTrack does not represent itself as the owner of that information.

3. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, the app may process the following information:

  • Academic information: Student results, examination points, profile information, and other academic details retrieved through HEMIS.
  • Login and session information: Information required to sign in to HEMIS and maintain your session, if you use the login features.
  • Locally stored information: Certain settings, session information, or cached academic data may be stored on your device to support app functionality and improve loading performance.
  • Technical information: If third-party services such as advertising, analytics, or crash reporting tools are integrated into the version you use, those services may process device identifiers, diagnostic information, usage events, or other information as described in their applicable privacy policies.

4. How Information Is Used

Information processed by the app is used, as applicable, to:

  • Retrieve and display academic information requested by you.
  • Calculate and analyze student results.
  • Maintain login sessions and remember app preferences.
  • Cache information locally to improve app performance.
  • Support advertising, analytics, or app diagnostics if the relevant services are included in the installed version.

5. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

UniTrack does not operate its own server for collecting academic information unless explicitly stated in a future version of this policy. Information you submit through HEMIS-related features may be transmitted to the HEMIS platform or its service providers as necessary to fulfill your request.

If advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or other third-party SDKs are included in the app, their providers may collect or process information in accordance with their own policies and configurations. Such processing may include advertising identifiers, usage information, or diagnostic data.

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed or disclosed where necessary to provide requested services, comply with applicable legal obligations, or protect the security of the app and its users.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Some information may be stored locally on your device to support app features, preferences, and caching. Information retained by HEMIS or a third-party service is subject to that service's own retention practices and policies.

You can clear locally stored app information by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app, where applicable. Uninstalling the app does not delete information retained by HEMIS or third-party service providers.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps within our control to protect information processed by the app. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. You should protect your login credentials and use the app on a device you trust.

8. Children's Privacy

UniTrack is intended for students who use academic services and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 for our own purposes. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us using the email address below.

9. Your Choices and Requests

You can stop using the app at any time and remove locally stored information by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it, where applicable. For information held by HEMIS or third-party providers, you may need to contact the relevant service directly.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features, services, or data practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
